SusPENse is not a regular desk organiser, but rather an extension to your desk, hidden entirely beneath your desk.
And while it is designed to be used in a classroom, it can also be used in day-to-day, to store laptops, drawing tablets, or even your coat!

What makes it special?

Other than the sick website?
Other than the amazing logo?
Other than the unique design?

It would probably be the practical day-to-day use, whether at home gaming and want to store your headset or at work and need to place your documents somewhere.
